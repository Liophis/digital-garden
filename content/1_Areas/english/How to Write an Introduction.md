## Purpose and Importance
### Answer Two Key Questions
A strong introduction justifies the existence of your research by answering: 
- **"Why was this study needed to fill the gap in scientific knowledge?"**
- **"Why does this gap need filling?"** (What is the significance or importance of this missing information?)

### The Logical Flow of Objectives:
To answer these questions effectively, the introduction should follow this logical progression:
- **Identify** the knowledge gap.
- **Explain** why it needs to be filled.
- **Summarize** how your study attempts to fill that gap.

### Introduction Content
1. Brief background information about research topic
2. Rationale for undertaking this study (reason for "filling the gap")
3. Key references to preliminary work or closely related papers
4. Clarification of terms, definitions, or abbreviations
5. Review of pertinent literature
-------
## The 3-Step Structure:

### Step 1: Establish What is Known (Background Information)
 To provide context and present the current state of knowledge in your research area.
1. **Start with a strong opening statement** that reflects your research subject area.
2.  **Provide highly relevant background information.** Avoid obvious or overly broad facts.
3.  **Review pertinent and up-to-date literature.** Cite key references and preliminary work to demonstrate your understanding of the field.
4.  **Use keywords from your title** to orient the reader.
5.  **Clarify essential terms, definitions, or abbreviations.**
6.  **Crucially, you must cite all sources referenced.**

### Step2: Show the gap in knowledge
To pinpoint the specific problem, question, or area of uncertainty that your research will address.
1. **Highlight specific areas where there is too little available information** or where current knowledge is insufficient.
2. **Explain why and how this gap should be filled.** Articulate its importance.
3. **Show that you have examined current data** and have a clear plan. This demonstrates your awareness of the field's direction.
4. **Propose the logical next steps** that can be developed from existing research, leading directly to your study.
5. **Convey confidence** in the purpose and direction of your study.

### Step3: Show how your study fills in the knowledge gaps(Purpose and Hypothesis)
To clearly state what your paper will do to address the gap and what you expect to find.
1. **State your purpose clearly.** Formulate the specific aim or objective of your study.
2. **Provide a clear hypothesis.** This is a concise (1-2 sentence) supposition or testable explanation.
3.  **Explain what useful knowledge will be gained** from your study, highlighting its contribution to the field.
----
## General Writing Tips

1. Write in the active voice 
2. Write concise sentences
3. Use stronger, more precise verbs
4. Don't overuse first-person pronouns to maintain an objective tone
5. Organize your thought from broad to specific, following the funnel structure described above
 
 - Write the introduction **after** you have completed the main body of the paper: **Materials & Methods, Results, and Discussion** and **before** you finalize the **Conclusion.**
