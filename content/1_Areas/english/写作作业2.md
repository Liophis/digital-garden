Figure 4.2 presents data on average age of mother at first birth in United Statas from 1970 to 2006. It is clear from the data that, on average, women in the U.S. have been progressively delaying having their first child throughout this period. To be specific, the average age at first birth was 21.4 years in 1970. By 2006, this figure had climbed to 25.0 years, increasing overally. This societal shift can likely be attributed to changing cultural and economic factors. As women may postpone marriage to pursue education and careers ( shown in Figure 4.1 ), the decision to have children was similarly delayed.
